SCHSREC x NHSRC End Of Year Day Trip to Wye River

Description:

After a successful year of collaboration between NHS and SCHS, a day trip seems adequate to celebrate! Join both clubs at Wye River via V/Line train and coach. Located along Victoria’s famous Great Ocean Road, this short trip is an exciting opportunity to marvel at the great sea. The trip will pass through the coastal city of Geelong, before going through major towns on the coast, including Torquay, Anglesea and Lorne. This will also be our last collaboration of the year, so join us for one last time before 2026 comes to an end!


On the way to Wye River, we will be taking a Geelong V/Line, changing over at Geelong Railway Station for a coach down the Otways to Wye River.
